# https://www.gstatic.com/ipranges/goog.json holds every address Google owns, its own services included (Analytics, Tag Manager, YouTube, Maps, Search), so it would name Google for services it serves itself. https://www.gstatic.com/ipranges/cloud.json holds the external addresses of customers' resources (https://docs.cloud.google.com/vpc/docs/configure-private-google-access), the regional addresses of Compute Engine VMs included (https://docs.cloud.google.com/vpc/docs/ip-addresses), so a site served from its own VM would pass for one behind Google. Neither is in an accepted format.
